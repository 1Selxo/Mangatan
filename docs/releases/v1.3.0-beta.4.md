# Mangatan v1.3.0 beta 4 (build 219)

Prerelease. Beta 4 replaces the manga reader with upstream Mangayomi's
rewritten reader and builds Mangatan's language-learning features back on
top of it. Settings carry over, but expect rough edges and report
regressions.

- Manga reader: upstream's v0.9.95 reader. Reading modes now include the
  direction (left to right, right to left, vertical, continuous, webtoon),
  with upstream's paging, zoom, double pages, crop, split pages and
  prefetching. Settings saved with the old separate direction fold into the
  mode, and the old cover-offset double page becomes a double page with a
  single first page.
- OCR and lookup work on the new reader in every mode: tap, hover and
  middle-button lookup, the lookup key, the two-finger toggle, the chapter
  scan from the page you are on with its progress, and the manual trigger.
  Boxes stay on their text when zoomed, with crop borders, and on split
  pages, where each half shows only its own text. Holding the middle button
  to look up no longer drags the page.
- Immersion statistics, E-Ink mode, the page preview grid (a button in the
  bottom bar) and panel-by-panel navigation are back. Panel navigation steps
  through the panels in reading order, animates on spreads too, and going
  back from a new page returns to the previous one.
- The "Automatic" background picks each page's colour, the way Mihon does,
  with a colour for each side: dark around dark pages, white around white
  ones. It fades between pages and stays under the colour filter.
- Reader: fit height now also works in vertical continuous and webtoon
  (each page as high as the screen), the white of a white-and-black flash
  shows, the mouse wheel turns pages and zooms toward the pointer, and the
  background colour is applied in every mode, under the colour filter.
- OCR: local ScreenAI shows text again, and the settings search finds the
  OCR and panel navigation settings.
- Detail page: no grey screen after selecting chapters or marking one
  unread.
- Lookup: the arrow keys keep working after the dictionary popup closes.
- Player: shortcut pills and the compact bar under the timeline, and mpv
  shaders take effect on desktop.
- Extension server: pairs with M-Extension-Server v1.0.8.1.

Full changelog: [v1.3.0-beta.3...v1.3.0-beta.4](https://github.com/1Selxo/Mangatan/compare/v1.3.0-beta.3...v1.3.0-beta.4).
