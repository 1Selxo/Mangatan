# Mangatan v1.3.0 beta 5 (build 220)

Prerelease. Beta 5 brings in upstream Mangayomi v0.9.96 and v0.9.97, and
makes reading progress carry over from Chimahon.

- Reading progress: a manga page read in Chimahon now opens at that page in
  Mangatan, from the chapter and from the resume button alike, and the chapter
  list shows it. Opening a chapter in Mangatan no longer overwrites newer
  progress from another device, and synced pages are no longer off by one.
- Sync: "Preparing sync…" shows as soon as Sync, Upload or Download only is
  tapped, then "Syncing…" until it finishes, in every language.
- Library: remembers the selected category, also after closing the app on
  desktop, and the last row no longer sits under the selection bar. The
  continue-reading button hides on series you are caught up on, and once a
  chapter is finished moves on to the first unread one.
- Missing chapters: the series page shows how many chapters are missing and
  lists them, and the manga and novel readers ask before skipping past a gap
  (this can be turned off in the reader settings).
- Downloads: the queue runs top to bottom, can be paused and resumed, and a
  download or a whole series can be moved to the top or bottom.
- Browse: tapping a genre on a series opens that genre in the source. Mihon
  sources whose filters load from the site, such as KissLove, now get their
  genre filters, and Reset in the filter sheet loads them again.
- Novel reader: the arrow keys turn EPUB pages as soon as the book opens. In
  the paged layouts, full-page pictures nested deep in the book's markup, and
  Kobo artwork, get a page of their own.
- Anki: the lookup popup's "already mined" check follows the profile's note
  type, as adding the card does.
- Appearance: an app-wide font, which also becomes the novel reader's
  "Default" font. Long-pressing the "In library" heart edits the series'
  categories.
- App lock keeps the page you were on when it locks and unlocks, and the
  memory overlay in General settings shows again. Pages no longer run under
  the system navigation bar, and the desktop window returns at its saved size
  without going fullscreen.
- iOS: the embedded Mihon runtime v17 carries the fixes of extension server
  v1.0.8.1.
- Extension server: pairs with M-Extension-Server v1.0.8.1.

Full changelog: [v1.3.0-beta.4...v1.3.0-beta.5](https://github.com/1Selxo/Mangatan/compare/v1.3.0-beta.4...v1.3.0-beta.5).
