# Mangatan v1.3.0 beta 3 (build 218)

Prerelease. Beta 3 adds upstream's redesigned manga reader and player
chrome and restores the iOS build, which failed for betas 1 and 2. It brings
in Mangayomi v0.9.95 and its Shiori redesign together with Mangatan's own
features, so expect rough edges and report regressions.

- Upstream Mangayomi v0.9.95: the Shiori design system and new transitions
  across the app, a rebuilt video player layout, swiping sideways between
  media in Library and Browse, a local tracker that needs no account, reader
  and player options (video filters, audio delay, webtoon slicing, wheel
  zoom), background downloads and syncs on Android, and many fixes.
- Manga reader: upstream's Shiori design. The bottom bar is one floating
  card with the page slider, the chapter list, a reading mode picker with
  tiles (and Mangatan's separate reading direction), crop, double page,
  page previews, auto-scroll and settings. Dragging the slider shows the
  page under it. The settings sheet uses grouped rows, with E-Ink mode and
  the OCR settings in their own places.
- Player: upstream's layout on phone and desktop. The timeline and the
  player's buttons float in one panel over the foot of the picture, the
  top bar names the series and episode, phones get a control lock, and
  holding the picture plays faster with a slide to pick the speed. The
  remembered volume and brightness settings now apply.
- Novel reader: import your own fonts (.ttf, .otf, .ttc) from the Custom
  entry in the font picker. Imported fonts are stored in app storage and
  apply to plain-text chapters and EPUBs, so Japanese fonts work in the EPUB
  reader. The preset fonts apply to plain-text chapters again.
- Novel reader: horizontal continuous EPUB reading keeps its layout and
  position, and Kobo kepub chapters open.
- Lookup and popups: the pointer can reach a hover popup across other text,
  and an open popup is replaced in place instead of flashing.
- OCR: overlay text is sized and placed where the engine saw it, and Mihon
  pages are found in the image cache again.
- Reader: quick, smooth double-tap zoom around the pointer.
- Player: faster subtitle hover lookups, and the selected subtitle track is
  the one mpv actually uses.
- Library and sources: Continue shelf tiles no longer overflow on CJK titles,
  file drops go to the visible tab only, titles restored before their
  extension was installed are rebound, and dictionaries stay enabled across
  sync and updates.
- iOS: pin the embedded runtime v16, and build the bridge from the commit
  that runtime came from, which brings the iOS build back. Bump the build
  number to 218.

Full changelog: [v1.2.26...v1.3.0-beta.3](https://github.com/1Selxo/Mangatan/compare/v1.2.26...v1.3.0-beta.3).
